## English — Apple App Review Notes

```text
Mili Bead Studio is a self-contained creative puzzle game designed for children ages 9–11.

ACCESS
- No account, sign-in, demo credentials, subscription, or network connection is required.
- Launch the app and tap “开始挑战” (Start Challenge) to begin.
- All 12 patterns and gameplay content are bundled in the app and work offline.

CORE FUNCTIONALITY
- The child follows an original 18×18 fuse-bead pattern using nine enlarged 6×6 touch zones.
- Progress and completed works are stored only in the app’s local container.
- Completing a pattern unlocks an in-app three-layer animation and a locally generated 1200×1500 PNG pattern/work card.

KIDS / PARENTAL GATE
- The app has no ads, in-app purchases, external links, chat, public profiles, leaderboard, or user-generated-content community.
- Saving, sharing, or printing the generated PNG is reserved for an adult. The adult must press and hold the action for 1.4 seconds and then solve a randomly generated three-digit-by-two-digit multiplication problem. Only after a correct answer does the app open the operating-system share sheet or print flow.
- To review this flow: open any pattern → tap “生成打印图” → press and hold “家长长按·保存或分享” → solve the displayed math problem → the system share sheet opens.
- The shared item is generated by the app from the selected bead pattern. The app does not accept a child’s photo, camera input, free-form text, voice, or other personal content.

PRIVACY
- The developer and integrated SDKs do not collect or transmit user or device data.
- There is no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking, account, cloud save, push notification, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo-library access.
- Gameplay progress remains on device. A parent can delete it from “家长与隐私说明” (Parent & Privacy Information) → “清除本机游戏记录” (Clear Local Game Records), or by uninstalling the app.
- When a parent explicitly chooses a destination in the system share sheet, the developer does not receive the generated file.

MINIMUM FUNCTIONALITY
This is not a remote website or content catalog. The signed app bundles its gameplay code and 12 patterns, works offline, validates each bead placement, persists progress, generates images locally, renders completion animations, and uses the native Filesystem/Share integration only behind the parental gate.

CONTENT RIGHTS
The 12 bead-pattern designs, their pixel grids, stories, color layouts, and motion-layer plans were created for this app. No third-party character, franchise, logo, or copied pattern is included.

SAFETY
The app states that real-world ironing and cutting must be performed by an adult.

The first release is not offered in China mainland.
```

## 中文 — Apple 审核备注

```text
米粒拼豆社是一款为 9–11 岁儿童设计的内置式创意拼豆游戏。

访问方式
- 无需账号、登录、演示凭据、订阅或网络连接。
- 启动后点击“开始挑战”即可进入。
- 12 张图纸与游戏内容全部随安装包提供，可离线运行。

核心功能
- 孩子按照原创 18×18 拼豆图纸，在九个放大的 6×6 触控分区中逐颗完成。
- 进度与完成作品仅保存在应用本机容器。
- 完成图纸后可播放应用内三层动画，并在本机生成 1200×1500 PNG 图纸或作品卡。

儿童与家长门
- 无广告、应用内购买、外部链接、聊天、公开资料、排行榜或用户内容社区。
- 保存、分享或打印生成的 PNG 仅供成年人操作。成年人必须先持续按住按钮 1.4 秒，再完成一道随机三位数乘以两位数的成人乘法题；答案正确后才会打开操作系统分享面板或打印流程。
- 复现路径：打开任一图纸 → 点击“生成打印图” → 长按“家长长按·保存或分享” → 解答题目 → 打开系统分享面板。
- 被分享的是应用根据拼豆图纸本机生成的图片。应用不接收儿童照片、相机输入、自由文本、语音或其他个人内容。

隐私
- 开发者与集成 SDK 均不收集或传输用户或设备数据。
- 无分析、广告、追踪、账号、云存档、推送、位置、相机、麦克风、通讯录或相册访问。
- 进度留在设备上。家长可以在“家长与隐私说明”中选择“清除本机游戏记录”，也可通过卸载应用删除。
- 家长在系统分享面板中主动选择目的地时，开发者不会收到该图片。

最低功能
本应用不是远程网站或内容目录。签名安装包内置游戏代码和 12 张图纸，可离线运行，逐颗校验拼制、保存进度、本机生成图片、播放完成动画，并仅在家长门后调用原生 Filesystem/Share。

内容权利
12 张拼豆图纸、像素网格、故事、配色与动画分层均为本应用原创，不含第三方角色、品牌、标志或复制图纸。

安全
应用明确提示实际熨烫与裁剪必须由成年人完成。

首发不在中国大陆地区提供。
```
